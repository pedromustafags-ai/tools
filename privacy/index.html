Privacy

Last updated 10/15/2026

These tools are run by Pedro Mustafa, who does marketing for small businesses under the name M&O.

What I collect

The numbers you type into a calculator, and the name, email, phone number and website you give me to get your report. I also record when you open the report and which parts you copy or print, so I know whether it was useful.

What I do with it

I send you the report you asked for, and a few follow-up emails and texts about it. I don't sell or share your information with anyone, and I don't add you to anyone else's list.

Texts

I only text if you check the box on the form. I text from my own phone, up to 5 times in the 10 days after you use a tool. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP and I won't text you again.

Getting out

Reply to any email or text and ask me to delete what you sent. I'll delete it and confirm.

Ads

If you came from an ad, the page may use the Meta pixel to tell the ad whether someone used the tool. It doesn't send your name, email or numbers.
