How this report is put together

The bet, the ratio and the map come from the article, quoted in its own words. The list of local businesses in chapter 03 and the five jobs in chapter 04 are our reading of the article's three tests, applied to businesses the article doesn't cover. They aren't a measurement, and your market can differ.

Your opening counts the jobs you said your customers still do themselves, out of the five we list for your trade. The first job to take over is the first of those in our order, which puts the work that repeats and follows a routine ahead of the work that takes judgement.
