# Draft review

Customers stop reading at the first line that sounds like a template. This skill finds those lines before a customer
does.

## Read first

`brand.md` (how the business talks and what it can prove) and `customer-words.md` (how customers talk). If the
sales-copy skill is installed, read its `references/ai-tells.md` too.

## Check, in this order

1. **Claims.** Every number, result, guarantee and review must come from `brand.md` or from the user. Mark anything
   else and ask about it.
2. **Customer words.** Where `customer-words.md` has a plainer phrase for the same thing, use it.
3. **Signs of machine-written text.**
   - A first half that only exists to be denied: "it's not about X, it's about Y" and its relatives. Keep the second
     half and give it a fact.
   - Stock words: delve, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, robust, leverage, "in today's fast-paced world".
   - Three adjectives in a row that say one thing.
   - A dash or a bold phrase in every paragraph.
   - An opening that announces the topic instead of starting it, and a closing slogan instead of a next step.
4. **Voice.** Read it aloud once. If the owner wouldn't say it to a customer at the door, rewrite it.

## Return

The draft with the changes applied, then a short list: what you changed and why, and every claim the user needs to
confirm. Never add a claim to rescue a weak line.
