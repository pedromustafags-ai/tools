# How they found you

The cheapest marketing data a small business can get is one question asked at the right moment.

## The question

Give the user this line to ask every new customer when they book, by phone or by message:

"Quick question before I go: how did you find us?"

If the answer is "Google", ask once more: "Do you remember what you searched for?"

## Logging

Each time the user pastes or tells you an answer, add one line to `found-you.md`:

date | customer first name or initials | their exact words | channel | the job, and its value if the user gives it

Channels: Google search, Google Maps, a referral, social media, a repeat customer, a sign or vehicle, other. Pick the
channel yourself only when it's obvious; otherwise ask.

## Once a month

Write a short report at the top of `found-you.md`:

- New customers this month, by channel, as counts. No percentages under 20 customers.
- The exact searches people remembered.
- The channel that brought the biggest jobs, if values were logged.
- One thing to do more of and one to stop, each with the line in the log that supports it.

Then tell the user to run customer-research with this month's answers.

## Rules

- Never estimate a channel the log doesn't show.
- With fewer than 10 new customers in a month, say the numbers are too few to decide anything yet.
