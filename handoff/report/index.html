How your number is worked out

Hours a week, times your rate, times 50 working weeks. For calls and quotes, half the hours count as hours you could hand off, because the first conversation with a new customer and the price stay with you.

What the number leaves out: the time it takes to train someone in the first weeks, and the hours that free up for selling or for the job itself. Your real number can land higher or lower.
