How your number is worked out

Requests that never got a price are counted at that same rate, so that part of the number comes straight from you.

For slow replies and quiet quotes the calculator assumes one thing: a request answered the next day, or a quote nobody followed up on, books less often than one handled on time. The research in chapter 05 puts the real cost of a late reply higher than that, so the assumption leans low on purpose. You know your customers better than any calculator. Move it and the whole report updates.

Assumption used in this copy: 25%.

What the number leaves out: calls that rang while you were on a job and never left a message, customers who came back on their own, and the reviews and referrals each job brings. Your real number can land higher or lower.
